TURAR

Turar Turganalieva, member of the Union of Artists of the Kyrgyz Republic, embroiders ornament that heals, guards and remembers — drawn in soap on cloth, finished in silk.

Selected Works

The Artist

Born beside the embroidery frame

An economist for twenty years, Turar left the ledgers at forty for the craft she had watched in her mother’s hands since childhood. Eight years later, the Union of Artists of Kyrgyzstan admitted the self-taught master.
